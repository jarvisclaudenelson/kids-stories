# Character Descriptions for Image Generation

Use these descriptions when generating story illustrations. The goal is **visual consistency across chapters**.

## Core reference assets

Use these as the main visual references whenever possible:

- `world/assets/nelson-boys-photo.jpg` — real reference photo of the boys
- `world/assets/boy-final.png` — stylized boy reference
- `world/assets/ollie-final.png` — stylized Ollie reference

## Important rules

- Keep the boys recognizable and age-appropriate
- Keep the same face shapes, hair direction, and overall silhouette from chapter to chapter
- Do **not** accidentally give Julian or Declan a fragment
- Do **not** move fragment placement around from image to image
- Prefer warm, cinematic, storybook 3D lighting over generic poster art

## Character descriptions

### Julian "Juju" Nelson (7)
- 7-year-old Caucasian boy
- Sandy blonde short-cropped hair
- Hazel-blue eyes
- Average / slightly athletic build
- Usually in colony clothes or dark blue colony jacket
- Often holding or near a worn notebook
- No fragment, no personal glow
- Expression: determined, analytical, brave

### Ezra "EZ" Nelson (5)
- 5-year-old Caucasian boy
- Dark brown medium-to-shoulder-length hair
- Brown eyes
- Light freckles
- Smaller, wiry build
- Usually in colony clothes or orange hoodie
- Gold heart-shaped fragment embedded in chest; glows through clothing
- Expression: empathetic, curious, emotionally tuned-in

### Declan "Decker" Nelson (2)
- 2-year-old Caucasian toddler boy
- Light / medium brown short wispy hair
- Brown eyes
- Chubby cheeks, sturdy toddler build
- Usually in green jumpsuit, pajamas, or toddler colony clothes
- No fragment, no personal glow
- Expression: solemn, curious, fearless, toddler-serious

### Ollie
- Medium-sized Schnoodle (schnauzer + poodle mix)
- Grey-silver / white scruffy curly coat
- Sturdy athletic build
- Blue collar
- Expression: loyal, alert, intelligent, protective

### Sable (15)
- 15-year-old girl
- Tan skin
- Dark hair pulled back, usually a ponytail
- Dark eyes
- Thin scar along jawline
- Practical dark traveler clothes
- Silver-blue fragment worn as pendant on cord
- Expression: composed, alert, focused

### Rafe (12)
- 12-year-old boy
- Red messy hair
- Pale skin with dark freckles
- Quiet, steady expression
- Casual / practical colony clothes
- Amber-orange fragment on cord around neck
- Expression: patient, observant, ready to help

### Petra (13)
- 13-year-old girl
- Short red hair, close-cropped at sides
- Weather-tanned pale skin
- Practical worn clothes / jacket with snack bars in pocket
- Iridescent violet-white fragment on cord around neck
- Expression: confident, resourceful, dryly amused

### Noa (12)
- 12-year-old girl
- Dark skin
- Close-cropped natural dark hair
- Large headphones around neck
- Oversized patched tan cargo jacket with sleeves rolled up
- Practical boots
- Yellow-green teardrop fragment on woven-wire cord around left wrist
- Expression: sharp, self-possessed, independent

## Prompt template

```text
Pixar/Disney-quality 3D animated illustration, warm cinematic lighting, rich saturated colors, expressive faces, 16:9 landscape format.

Scene: [describe the page beat clearly]

Characters:
- Julian: 7-year-old Caucasian boy, sandy blonde short-cropped hair, hazel-blue eyes, colony clothes, worn notebook nearby, no fragment
- Ezra: 5-year-old Caucasian boy, dark brown medium-length hair, brown eyes, freckles, orange hoodie or colony clothes, gold heart-shaped fragment glowing through chest
- Declan: 2-year-old Caucasian toddler boy, light brown wispy hair, brown eyes, green jumpsuit or toddler colony clothes, no fragment
- Ollie: medium-sized grey-silver scruffy Schnoodle with blue collar
- [Add Sable / Rafe / Petra / Noa as needed]

No text bubbles. No captions in the image.
```

## Illustration cadence

- Prefer page-level prompts for each chapter
- Capture the best visual beats rather than forcing art onto every paragraph
- Typical targets:
  - opening / establishing image
  - reveal or action image
  - emotional image
  - end beat / cliffhanger image
